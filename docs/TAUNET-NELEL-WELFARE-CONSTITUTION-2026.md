# ARTICLE 1 - NAME

## Name of the Association

The name of the Association shall be:

TAUNET NELEL WELFARE ASSOCIATION - VICTORIA

hereinafter referred to in this Constitution as "the Welfare Association" or "the Association."

## Status

The Welfare Association is a welfare arm operating under Taunet Nelel Incorporated and exists to provide financial, social and welfare support to its eligible members in accordance with this Constitution.

## Legal Identity

The Welfare Association shall operate in accordance with:

- this Constitution;
- the Constitution of Taunet Nelel Incorporated;
- all applicable Commonwealth and Victorian legislation; and
- any lawful resolutions adopted by members at a General Meeting.

## Official Motto

The official motto of the Association shall be:

"Together We Stand, Divided We Fall."

This motto shall guide every activity, decision and objective of the Association.

# ARTICLE 2 - INTERPRETATION AND DEFINITIONS

## Interpretation

Unless the context otherwise requires, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings assigned to them in this Constitution.

## Definitions

### Annual General Meeting (AGM)

Means the meeting of members held each year in accordance with this Constitution for conducting the business of the Association.

### Association

Means Taunet Nelel Welfare Association - Victoria.

### Beneficiary

Means a person nominated by a member or otherwise recognised under this Constitution to receive welfare assistance following an eligible welfare event.

### Committee

Means the elected Welfare Committee responsible for the administration and management of the Association.

### Constitution

Means this Constitution together with all amendments lawfully adopted by members.

### Director

Means the elected Director of the Welfare Association.

### Financial Member

Means a registered member who:

- has paid all applicable membership fees;
- has complied with all financial obligations;
- is not suspended; and
- remains eligible to enjoy benefits under this Constitution.

### Financial Year

Means the period commencing 1 July and ending 30 June each year.

### General Meeting

Means any meeting of members convened in accordance with this Constitution.

### Member

Means a registered member of Taunet Nelel Incorporated who has been admitted into Taunet Nelel Welfare Association and who continues to meet all membership obligations.

### Office Bearers

Means the elected officers of the Welfare Committee including:

- Director;
- Secretary;
- Treasurer;
- Welfare Coordinators; and
- Committee Members.

### Ordinary Resolution

Means a resolution passed by a simple majority of members present and entitled to vote.

### Special Resolution

Means a resolution requiring at least two-thirds of eligible members voting at a General Meeting.

### Welfare Event

Means an event recognised under this Constitution for which welfare assistance may be provided, including but not limited to:

- death of a member;
- death of an eligible beneficiary;
- approved hospitalisation; or
- any other welfare circumstance approved under this Constitution.

## References

Unless the context indicates otherwise:

- Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.
- Words importing one gender include every gender.
- References to legislation include amendments and replacements.
- Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.
- References to Articles refer to Articles contained in this Constitution.

## Governing Authority

The Committee shall interpret this Constitution in good faith.

Where uncertainty exists, the matter shall be determined by members at a General Meeting.

# ARTICLE 7 - MEMBERSHIP

## Eligibility

Membership of Taunet Nelel Welfare Association shall be available only to registered members of Taunet Nelel Incorporated who satisfy the requirements of this Constitution. This maintains the eligibility requirement contained in the original constitution.

## Admission

A person shall become a member upon:

- being a registered member of Taunet Nelel Incorporated;
- completing the prescribed membership process;
- agreeing to comply with this Constitution;
- paying the prescribed membership fee under Article 9; and
- being entered into the official Register of Members.

## Membership Commencement

Membership shall commence on the date the Committee approves the application or such other date determined by the Committee.

## Waiting Period for Benefits

A newly admitted member shall complete a three (3) month waiting period before becoming eligible to receive welfare benefits under this Constitution.

The waiting period commences from the official date of admission into the Welfare Association.

## Reimbursement Obligations During Waiting Period

Notwithstanding Article 7.4, where a reimbursable welfare event occurs after a person has become a member but before completion of the three (3) month waiting period, that member shall be required to contribute any reimbursement levy or special welfare contribution approved under this Constitution in the same manner as every other financial member.

However:

- the member shall not be entitled to receive welfare benefits for their own claim until the expiry of the three (3) month waiting period;
- payment of reimbursement contributions during the waiting period shall not shorten or waive the waiting period;
- all other eligibility requirements under this Constitution shall continue to apply.

## Membership Rights

Financial members shall have the right to:

- participate in meetings;
- vote at General Meetings;
- nominate for eligible offices;
- receive welfare benefits subject to eligibility;
- inspect approved Association records in accordance with this Constitution.

## Membership Responsibilities

Every member shall:

- comply with this Constitution;
- pay all prescribed fees and levies when due;
- notify the Association of changes to their contact details;
- uphold the reputation of the Association;
- cooperate with Committee investigations where required.

## Resignation

A member may resign by giving not less than twenty-eight (28) days' written notice to the Secretary.

Before resignation becomes effective:

- all outstanding financial obligations shall be settled; and
- any Association property shall be returned.

## Withdrawal from Welfare

A member may withdraw from the Welfare Association while remaining a member of Taunet Nelel Incorporated.

Any person who subsequently wishes to rejoin the Welfare Association shall:

- submit a new application;
- satisfy all membership requirements applicable at the time of re-admission;
- pay the prescribed membership fee; and
- complete a new three (3) month waiting period unless otherwise determined by a Special Resolution of members.

## Suspension or Termination

Membership may be suspended or terminated where a member:

- seriously breaches this Constitution;
- knowingly provides false information;
- fails to meet financial obligations after reasonable notice;
- engages in conduct detrimental to the Association.

The affected member shall be afforded procedural fairness, including an opportunity to respond before any final decision is made.

# ARTICLE 8 - MEMBERSHIP THRESHOLD FOR COMMENCEMENT OF WELFARE OPERATIONS

## Minimum Membership Threshold

The Welfare Association shall commence the payment of welfare benefits only after attaining the minimum membership threshold approved by members.

The minimum membership threshold required for the commencement of welfare operations shall be:

Sixty (60) registered and financial members.

This provision preserves the original constitutional requirement while recognising that the threshold has since been achieved by the Association.

The Committee shall not approve or disburse welfare benefits unless the Association has:

- attained the minimum membership threshold;
- established an operational Welfare Fund;
- opened an approved Association bank account;
- appointed authorised signatories; and
- adopted appropriate financial management procedures.

## Unlimited Membership

There shall be no maximum limit on the number of members who may join the Welfare Association.

Membership shall remain open throughout the year, subject to compliance with this Constitution and any administrative procedures adopted by the Committee.

## Sustainability

The Committee shall continually monitor:

- membership growth;
- financial reserves;
- projected liabilities; and
- long-term sustainability

to ensure that welfare obligations can continue to be met without jeopardising the financial stability of the Association.

## Membership Register

The Association shall maintain an accurate Register of Members containing:

- full name;
- membership number (where applicable);
- date of admission;
- contact details;
- payment status; and
- beneficiary details.

The Register shall be maintained by the Secretary and kept confidential in accordance with Article 22 of this Constitution.

# ARTICLE 9 - MEMBERSHIP FEES AND CONTRIBUTIONS

## Membership Fee

Every eligible applicant admitted into the Welfare Association shall pay a non-refundable membership fee of AUD $300.00 (Three Hundred Australian Dollars).

The membership fee is a one-off payment and is payable only once upon admission into the Welfare Association. This maintains the original membership fee requirement.

## Payment Period

The membership fee shall be paid:

- in full upon joining; or
- by instalments completed within ninety (90) days from the date of admission.

A member shall not become eligible for welfare benefits until:

- the full membership fee has been paid; and
- the three (3) month waiting period has expired.

## Instalment Payment Arrangement

The Association recognises that some applicants may require flexibility in meeting the membership fee.

Accordingly:

- members may pay the fee progressively over ninety (90) days;
- the Committee may approve structured payment arrangements;
- all payments shall be recorded in the Association's financial records.

## Approved Payment Options

The Welfare Association may accept payments through approved methods including:

- Direct Bank Transfer;
- Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT);
- Debit or Credit Card;
- Afterpay;
- Zip Pay;
- Klarna; and
- any other payment platform approved by the Committee.

The Committee may amend available payment methods without requiring an amendment to this Constitution.

## Monthly Payment Option

Where approved by the Committee, an applicant may contribute AUD $100.00 per month until the full membership fee has been paid within the ninety (90) day payment period.

Failure to complete payment within the prescribed period may result in suspension or cancellation of the membership application unless an extension is approved by the Committee.

## Reimbursement Levies

In addition to the membership fee, every financial member shall contribute reimbursement levies or welfare contributions approved by the Committee following an eligible welfare event.

Such levies shall:

- be payable by every financial member;
- become due within the period stated on the invoice;
- be used exclusively for approved welfare purposes.

## Due Date for Welfare Invoices

Unless otherwise determined by the Committee, reimbursement invoices shall be payable within fourteen (14) days from the date of issue.

Members who fail to pay within the prescribed period may:

- lose access to welfare benefits;
- have their membership suspended;
- incur any other sanctions approved under this Constitution.

## Outstanding Contributions

A member who has outstanding welfare contributions shall not be eligible to receive welfare benefits until all outstanding obligations have been settled, unless the Committee determines that exceptional circumstances justify alternative arrangements.

## Refunds

Membership fees shall not be refunded upon:

- resignation;
- termination of membership;
- suspension; or
- voluntary withdrawal from the Welfare Association.

Any reimbursement or welfare levy already paid shall likewise be non-refundable except where the Committee has collected an amount in error.

## Review of Fees

The membership fee and reimbursement levy structure shall be reviewed periodically by the Committee.

Any proposal to increase or reduce the membership fee shall require approval by an Ordinary Resolution at a General Meeting.

# ARTICLE 11 - WELFARE BENEFITS AND ASSISTANCE

## Purpose of Welfare Benefits

The Welfare Association shall provide financial assistance to eligible financial members in accordance with this Constitution during approved welfare events.

All welfare payments shall be made from the Welfare Fund and shall be subject to the availability of funds, verification of eligibility, and approval by the Welfare Committee.

## Death of a Member

Where a financial member of the Welfare Association dies, the Association shall provide financial assistance of AUD $12,000.00 (Twelve Thousand Australian Dollars) to assist the deceased member's nominated beneficiary or legal next of kin with funeral and related expenses. Payment shall only be made after verification by the Committee.

## Death of a Parent

Where a financial member loses either:

- biological father;
- biological mother;
- legally adopted parent; or
- legal guardian recognised by the Committee,

the member shall receive welfare assistance of AUD $10,000.00 (Ten Thousand Australian Dollars) subject to verification of the claim.

## Death of a Sibling

Where a financial member loses a biological or legally adopted sibling, the member shall receive welfare assistance of AUD $8,000.00 (Eight Thousand Australian Dollars) upon approval of the Welfare Committee.

## Eligible Beneficiaries

For the purposes of this Constitution, benefits shall only be payable to:

- financial members;
- their nominated beneficiaries;
- their legally recognised next of kin; and
- any other person approved by the Committee where exceptional circumstances exist.

## Waiting Period

A member shall not qualify for benefits under Articles 11.2, 11.3 or 11.4 until:

- completion of the three (3) month waiting period;
- payment of the full membership fee; and
- compliance with all financial obligations.

## Reimbursement Contribution During Waiting Period

Where a person joins the Welfare Association and, before completion of the three (3) month waiting period, another eligible member experiences a welfare event requiring reimbursement by members:

- the newly admitted member shall be required to pay the reimbursement levy in the same manner as every other financial member;
- payment of the reimbursement levy shall not create eligibility to receive welfare benefits;
- the member shall remain subject to the full three (3) month waiting period before becoming eligible to lodge any claim under this Constitution; and
- the Committee shall have no discretion to waive this waiting period except where expressly authorised by a Special Resolution of members.

## Hospitalisation Benefit

A financial member admitted to hospital shall be eligible for welfare assistance as follows:

### Hospitalisation between seven (7) and fourteen (14) consecutive days

The member shall receive AUD $1,000.00 provided that:

- the admission is verified;
- a medical certificate or discharge summary is provided; and
- the Committee confirms eligibility.

### Hospitalisation exceeding fourteen (14) consecutive days

Where a financial member remains admitted for more than fourteen (14) consecutive days, the member shall receive AUD $2,000.00 subject to:

- satisfactory medical evidence;
- verification by the Committee; and
- compliance with this Constitution.

These hospitalisation benefits preserve the existing constitutional entitlements.

## Verification of Claims

Before approving any welfare payment, the Committee may require:

- Death Certificate;
- Medical Certificate;
- Hospital Discharge Summary;
- Burial Permit;
- Letter from relevant authorities;
- Statutory Declaration; and
- any additional documentation reasonably required.

## Time for Lodging Claims

Claims should be lodged as soon as reasonably practicable after the welfare event.

The Committee may accept late claims where sufficient reasons are provided.

## Method of Payment

Approved welfare assistance shall be paid by:

- Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT);
- Direct Bank Transfer; or
- any secure payment method approved by the Committee.

Cash payments shall be avoided except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Committee.

## Fraudulent Claims

A member who knowingly submits false information or fraudulent documents:

- shall immediately lose entitlement to the claim;
- may have their membership suspended or terminated;
- may be required to repay monies received; and
- may be referred to the appropriate legal authorities where applicable.

## Committee Discretion

The Committee may request additional information before approving a claim but shall not vary the approved benefit amounts unless authorised by a General Meeting.

# ARTICLE 12 - WELFARE COMMITTEE OF MANAGEMENT

## Establishment

The Welfare Association shall be governed by a Welfare Committee of Management responsible for the administration and management of the affairs of the Association.

## Composition

The Welfare Committee shall consist of:

- Director
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Welfare Coordinator
- Welfare Coordinator

The Committee may appoint sub-committees where necessary. This maintains the original governance structure.

## Authority

The Committee shall exercise all powers necessary for the proper administration of the Welfare Association except those reserved to members under this Constitution.

## General Responsibilities

The Committee shall:

- administer this Constitution;
- manage the Welfare Fund;
- approve welfare payments;
- oversee membership administration;
- prepare financial reports;
- maintain proper records; and
- implement resolutions passed by members.

## Fiduciary Duties

Every Committee member shall:

- act honestly;
- act in the best interests of the Association;
- avoid conflicts of interest;
- exercise reasonable care and diligence;
- maintain confidentiality; and
- comply with this Constitution.

## Conflict of Interest

Where a Committee member has a personal interest in any matter before the Committee:

- the interest shall be declared;
- the declaration shall be recorded in the minutes;
- the member shall not vote on that matter; and
- the remaining Committee members shall determine the matter.

## Quorum

A quorum for Committee meetings shall be a simple majority of Committee members.

No decision shall be made without a quorum.

## Meetings

The Committee shall meet as often as necessary to discharge its responsibilities.

Meetings may be held:

- in person;
- by telephone;
- by video conference; or
- by any electronic platform approved by the Committee.

## Decision-Making

Committee decisions shall ordinarily be made by majority vote.

Where votes are equal, the Director shall have a casting vote.

## Delegation

The Committee may delegate administrative functions to:

- the Secretary;
- the Treasurer;
- Coordinators; or
- a sub-committee,

provided that responsibility for the delegated function ultimately remains with the Committee.

## Accountability

The Committee shall remain accountable to the members of the Welfare Association and shall present annual reports at each Annual General Meeting.

# ARTICLE 16 - REVIEW AND AMENDMENT OF THE CONSTITUTION

## Authority to Amend

This Constitution may be amended only by the members of Taunet Nelel Welfare Association at a duly convened General Meeting.

No amendment shall take effect unless it has been approved in accordance with this Article. This preserves the original constitutional provision requiring member approval for amendments.

## Who May Propose an Amendment

A proposal to amend this Constitution may be submitted by:

- the Welfare Committee;
- the Director;
- not fewer than five (5) financial members acting jointly; or
- a Special Committee established by members.

## Notice of Proposed Amendments

All proposed amendments shall:

- be submitted in writing to the Secretary;
- clearly identify the Article(s) to be amended;
- include the wording of the proposed amendment; and
- state the reasons for the proposed amendment.

The Secretary shall circulate the proposed amendment to members not less than fourteen (14) days before the meeting at which it will be considered.

## Voting Requirements

A proposed amendment shall be adopted where it receives the support of not less than two-thirds (2/3) of the financial members present and entitled to vote at a General Meeting.

Voting may be conducted:

- by secret ballot;
- electronically where approved by the Committee; or
- by any other lawful voting method approved by members.

## Recording Amendments

Upon approval:

- the Secretary shall update the Constitution;
- the amendment shall be dated;
- a record of all amendments shall be maintained; and
- the revised Constitution shall be made available to members.

## Periodic Review

The Committee shall review this Constitution at least once every three (3) years, or earlier where circumstances require, to ensure that it remains relevant, effective, and consistent with the objectives of the Association.

## Compliance with Law

No amendment shall be adopted if it is inconsistent with:

- applicable Commonwealth or Victorian legislation;
- the Constitution of Taunet Nelel Incorporated; or
- the not-for-profit objectives of the Welfare Association.

## Transitional Arrangements

Where an amendment affects the administration of the Association:

- existing decisions lawfully made before the amendment shall remain valid;
- the Committee shall implement appropriate transitional arrangements; and
- members shall be informed of any operational changes arising from the amendment.

# ARTICLE 18 - FEEDBACK AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

## Member Feedback

The Welfare Association recognises the importance of member participation and encourages constructive feedback to improve its governance, welfare services, and overall operations.

Members may submit suggestions, concerns, or recommendations to the Committee at any time. This expands on the original constitutional provision establishing a feedback mechanism.

## Submission of Feedback

Feedback may be submitted:

- in writing to the Secretary;
- by email;
- during Committee meetings;
- during General Meetings; and
- through any official communication channel approved by the Committee.

## Consideration of Feedback

The Committee shall:

- acknowledge receipt of feedback where practicable;
- consider all submissions fairly;
- determine whether action is required; and
- communicate significant outcomes to members where appropriate.

## Complaints

A member may lodge a formal complaint concerning:

- administration of the Welfare;
- Committee decisions;
- financial management;
- conduct of Committee members; and
- alleged breaches of this Constitution.

Complaints shall be submitted in writing to the Secretary.

## Internal Resolution

The Committee shall make reasonable efforts to resolve disputes internally through:

- discussion;
- mediation;
- consultation with affected parties; and
- any other appropriate dispute resolution process.

## Conflict of Interest

A Committee member who is the subject of a complaint or has a personal interest in a dispute:

- shall disclose the conflict;
- shall withdraw from deliberations on that matter; and
- shall not vote on the matter.

## Mediation

Where a dispute cannot be resolved internally, the parties may agree to refer the matter to an independent mediator before considering any legal proceedings.

## Final Decision

Subject to applicable law, the decision of members at a General Meeting on matters arising under this Constitution shall be final.

# ARTICLE 19 - ETHICS, CONFIDENTIALITY AND STANDARDS OF CONDUCT

## Ethical Principles

The Welfare Association shall conduct its affairs in accordance with the principles of:

- integrity;
- accountability;
- transparency;
- fairness;
- respect;
- compassion; and
- professionalism.

This builds upon the original ethical considerations contained in the Constitution.

## Code of Conduct

All members and Committee members shall:

- act honestly and respectfully;
- uphold the objectives of the Association;
- avoid conduct that may bring the Association into disrepute;
- comply with this Constitution; and
- respect decisions lawfully made under this Constitution.

## Confidentiality

Committee members shall maintain confidentiality regarding:

- welfare claims;
- medical information;
- financial information;
- personal information;
- Committee deliberations; and
- any confidential records of the Association.

Confidential information shall not be disclosed except:

- with the consent of the affected person;

The Association shall take reasonable steps to protect members' personal information and manage it in accordance with applicable Australian privacy legislation.

- this Constitution;

- applicable Australian laws.

# ARTICLE 20 - DISSOLUTION OF THE ASSOCIATION

## Dissolution

The Welfare Association may only be dissolved by a Special Resolution passed at a duly convened General Meeting.

The resolution shall receive the support of not less than two-thirds (2/3) of the financial members present and entitled to vote.

## Notice

Members shall receive not less than twenty-one (21) days' written notice of any meeting convened to consider dissolution.

The notice shall include:

- the reasons for the proposed dissolution;
- details of the Association's financial position; and
- proposed arrangements for the disposal of assets.

## Settlement of Liabilities

Before dissolution, the Committee shall ensure that:

- all lawful debts are paid;
- all approved welfare obligations are settled;
- outstanding contractual obligations are discharged; and
- final financial statements are prepared.

## Distribution of Remaining Assets

Following payment of all liabilities, any remaining assets shall not be distributed among members.

Instead, the remaining assets shall be transferred to:

- Taunet Nelel Incorporated; or
- another not-for-profit organisation with similar objectives, as determined by members at the meeting approving the dissolution.

## Preservation of Records

Upon dissolution, the Committee shall ensure that:

- financial records;
- minutes;
- membership registers;
- constitutional records; and
- other official documents

are retained or transferred in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

## Final Report

The Committee shall prepare a final report outlining:

- the reasons for dissolution;
- settlement of liabilities;
- disposal of assets;
- final financial statements; and
- completion of the dissolution process.

## Effect of Dissolution

Upon completion of the dissolution process:

- the Welfare Association shall cease to operate;
- all authority of the Committee shall terminate, except as required to finalise outstanding legal obligations; and
- the Constitution shall cease to have effect.
