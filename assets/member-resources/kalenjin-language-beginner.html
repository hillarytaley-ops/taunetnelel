Kalenjin language — beginner notes

Starter phrases for families practising Kalenjin at home. Expand this pack as Language School materials are added.

Greetings

Chamge / Chamgei
Hello / greetings
Ameiye?
How are you?
Ameiye mising
I am fine
Kongoi
Thank you

Tips

  • Practise one greeting a day with children.
  • Attend community language sessions when advertised on Events.
  • Ask elders for local dialect notes — Kalenjin has several varieties.
