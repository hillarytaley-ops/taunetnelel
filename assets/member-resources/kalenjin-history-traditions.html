Kalenjin history & traditions

A short cultural briefing for youth and new members of Taunet Nelel in Victoria.

Who we are

Taunet Nelel is a community organisation of Kalenjin-speaking Kenyans and friends in Australia. We celebrate heritage through family-friendly, alcohol-free events, language, and mutual support.

Community values

  • Respect for elders and family
  • Education and cultural continuity for the next generation
  • Multicultural harmony in Victoria
  • Motto: Together We Stand, Divided We Fall
