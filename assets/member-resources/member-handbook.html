Member Handbook 2026

Welcome to Taunet Nelel. This short handbook explains how the new members portal works.

Sign in

Use Members → Sign in with the email on the association list. New members can use Join Us with the same email.

Association vs Welfare

Association membership and Social Welfare are separate. You may hold one or both. Welfare features only appear when your welfare membership is active.

Dashboard

Your dashboard shows renewals, community events, announcements, and quick links to Resources and Welfare.

Need help?

Use Support or email info@taunetnelel.org.
